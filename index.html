Reworking of the Folliccanese Project

With regards to the Folliccanese Project, it is currently being redone. With the creation of Minorian, I have come to dislike the state of Folliccanese. I am currently working on Old Folliccanese; I plan on it being an agglutinative language, unlike the fusional language it was before (this is partially because Minorian grammar, a fusional grammar, was hell to create and I ain't trying to do all that again). After Old Folliccanes, I'll probably make a couple minor changes to it in order to create Classical Folliccanese, at which point I'll probably make a full showcase or something. I do plan on making an animated series involving the Follicles sometime in the near-ish future. I'll keep the old calculator thing up here because I kinda don't know what to do with this space.
